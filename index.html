How a project runs

Creative work goes better inside a clear frame: references, deliverables, and revision rounds agreed up front, so the project actually finishes and you know exactly what you're getting.

In scope

  • Kickoff call covering references, goals, release plans, and deadline
  • Songwriting or co-writing from your idea, lyric, melody, or brief
  • Arrangement and production, from sketch to full instrumentation
  • Vocal editing—comping, timing, and tuning on recorded takes
  • Mixing with a set number of revision rounds, agreed up front
  • Mastering for streaming, download, and video
  • Delivery: WAV and MP3 masters, instrumental and a cappella versions, stems on request

What you get

  • A finished, release-ready track—not a folder of loops and rough bounces
  • Ownership, splits, and usage rights in writing before work starts
  • A revision process with a finish line
  • Files named and organized for distributors, collaborators, and sync libraries
  • Direct access to who's producing it—no studio middleman

Packages

Every project is quoted as a flat price per song or per deliverable, based on where it starts and how finished you need it—so the bill doesn't grow with studio hours.
